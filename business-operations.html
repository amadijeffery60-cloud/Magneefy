The approach

When everything routes through the owner, growth stalls. We take on the coordination, administration and day-to-day operational work, and we build the structure (documented processes, organised workspaces, clear ownership) that keeps things running without constant supervision.

Questions

Common questions.

How is this different from hiring a freelance assistant?

You get operational thinking, not just task completion. Alongside daily support, we document processes, organise your systems and connect them with automation where it saves time.

Which tools do you work with?

Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Notion, Slack, Asana, Trello, ClickUp, HubSpot and most common CRMs and booking tools. If you use something else, we’ll learn it.

How do you handle confidential information?

Access is granted through your own accounts with the minimum permissions needed, and we’re happy to work under an NDA.

Can support scale up or down?

Yes. Support is arranged around agreed hours or responsibilities and can be adjusted as your needs change.
