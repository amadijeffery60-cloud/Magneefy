Our process

A clear path from bottleneck to better.

No guesswork and no unnecessary complexity. A structured way of understanding your business, fixing what matters most first, and improving continuously.

01STEP

Discover

We start with your business, not our services: your goals, customers, team, tools, and where time and opportunities are currently being lost.

02STEP

Diagnose & Plan

We identify the bottlenecks that matter most and turn them into a prioritised, practical roadmap with clear outcomes and scope.

03STEP

Build & Implement

We set up campaigns, automations, systems and support, testing everything properly and documenting how it works.

04STEP

Run, Measure & Improve

We manage, monitor and report in plain English, then keep refining what works and fixing what doesn’t.

In detail

What happens at each stage.

Step 01
Week 1

Discover

A focused conversation about your business, goals, customers and current tools, followed by a look at your existing marketing, systems and workflows.

  • Discovery call
  • Access to relevant accounts and tools
  • Review of current marketing, systems and processes
Step 02
Week 1–2

Diagnose & Plan

We identify the bottlenecks costing you the most time and opportunity, then translate them into a prioritised roadmap with clear scope and success measures.

  • Findings summary in plain English
  • Prioritised roadmap: quick wins first
  • Clear scope, timeline and investment
Step 03
Week 2 onward

Build & Implement

We set up the campaigns, automations, websites and support structures in the roadmap, testing each one and documenting how it works.

  • Builds in your accounts, owned by you
  • Testing before anything goes live
  • Documentation and team walkthroughs
Step 04
Ongoing

Run, Measure & Improve

Once live, we manage, monitor and report. Each review leads to the next set of improvements so results compound over time.

  • Regular reporting against agreed goals
  • Monitoring and fixes
  • A roadmap that evolves with the business

Working together

How we keep things running smoothly.

How do we communicate?

We agree on channels and rhythm at the start, usually email or Slack for day-to-day work, plus scheduled check-ins for reviews and planning.

Do you work in our time zone?

We work with businesses in the US and UK and schedule support and meetings around your business hours.

Who owns the accounts and systems you build?

You do. Ad accounts, automations, websites and documentation are set up in your name so you keep full control.

What do you need from us to get started?

A discovery conversation, access to the relevant tools, and a point of contact for questions. We keep the time we need from you to a minimum.
