Questions

Common questions.

Make.com or Zapier: which is better?

It depends on the complexity, volume and budget. Zapier is quick to set up and great for simpler connections; Make.com handles complex, multi-branch logic more cost-effectively. We recommend based on your workflows, not preference.

Will automation replace our team?

The aim is to remove repetitive work, not people. Automation gives your team back time for the judgement, relationships and problem-solving that actually grow the business.

Is AI safe to use with customer data?

We design workflows with data access kept to what’s necessary, use reputable platforms, and keep a human review step wherever accuracy or sensitivity matters.

What happens if an automation breaks?

Every build includes error handling and notifications. Ongoing support plans cover monitoring, fixes and updates as your tools change.
