About Magneefy

Grow Smarter. Operate Better.

Magneefy helps growing businesses attract more customers, improve conversions, automate repetitive work, and build efficient business operations.

Why we exist

Growing businesses are often stuck between two problems: not enough of the right customers, and not enough time or structure to serve the ones they have.

Most agencies solve one side. A marketing agency brings in leads that nobody follows up on. An operations consultant streamlines a business that still struggles to grow. A freelancer completes tasks without seeing how they connect.

Magneefy was built to close that gap. We combine digital marketing, AI automation, business operations support and customer experience under one roof, so the way you attract customers and the way you serve them are designed together.

THE MAGNEEFY BELIEF

Most business owners don’t need more tools or more advice. They need a partner who understands how the pieces connect, and who will do the work to make them run.

Jeffery Odiba founded Magneefy to give growing businesses one dependable partner across marketing, automation and day-to-day operations, so owners can stop holding everything together themselves and focus on the decisions that move the business forward.

Jeffery’s background spans executive virtual assistance, business operations management and hands-on digital delivery: running inboxes and calendars, documenting processes, building automations, managing campaigns and launching websites. That combination is the foundation of Magneefy’s approach: growth only lasts when the operations behind it can keep up.
